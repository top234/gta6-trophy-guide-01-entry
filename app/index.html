Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough is enough, about 200 hours. There is no mission replay and cheats disable trophies, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. Only one trophy can be missed — it is flagged below.

⚠ Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept the meditation. That one never comes back.

    About this guide

    Names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them; 28 trophies are hidden on PlayStation until earned. Every guide is written in this guide’s own words and names its sources beside it — the facts come from the PowerPyx GTA 6 trophy guide and roadmap and the CryptoGames trophy list, which agree on all 55 names. Five trophies are still unconfirmed by those sources and say so in their guide. No Rockstar artwork or logo is used anywhere: the look is our own. Progress is saved only in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back.
